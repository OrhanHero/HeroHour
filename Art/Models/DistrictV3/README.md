# Original CityHero district kit

Generated by `Scripts/art/build_district_models.py` in Blender 5.2. Original geometry; no extracted commercial game models. Units are metres, export forward X/up Z, ground pivot at origin. Explicit cube-projected UVs support tiling materials. All assets use the stable slots `Plaster, Concrete, Asphalt, Glass, Metal, Dust, Paint` in that order.

The three civic silhouettes share a compact ground footprint and provide storefronts, framed windows, terrace slabs, rooftop equipment and stepped upper volumes. They deliberately retain simple repeatable facade elements for the RTS camera rather than modelling unseen interiors.
